Albania Residence Permit — Complete Guide for Foreign Nationals

Albanian law requires anyone staying longer than 90 days to hold a valid residence permit. We guide Turkish and Azerbaijani citizens through every step — from choosing the right permit type to receiving approval.

Who Needs a Residence Permit in Albania?

Turkish and Azerbaijani citizens can enter Albania visa-free and stay up to 90 days within any 180-day period. If you plan to live, work, study, or do business in Albania beyond that threshold, you must apply for a residence permit before your visa-free period expires.

Failing to apply in time can result in fines or complications with future applications, so it is important to start the process early — ideally within the first month of arrival.

Types of Albania Residence Permit

Albania issues several categories of temporary residence permit, each tied to a specific purpose:

  • Employment-based — issued alongside a work permit as the unique permit (one document covering both)
  • Self-employment / freelance — for those running their own business or registered as a sole trader
  • Family reunification — for spouses and minor children joining a permit holder
  • Property ownership — purchasing real estate in Albania is a qualifying basis for residence
  • Pensioner / passive income — for retirees or those with sufficient passive income
  • Study — for students enrolled at an Albanian institution

Temporary residence permits are typically issued for one year and are renewable annually. After five years of continuous legal residence you may be eligible for a long-term permit.

Required Documents

The exact document list varies by permit category, but most applications require:

  • Valid passport (and copies of all pages)
  • Completed application form
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Proof of accommodation in Albania (rental contract or property title)
  • Proof of financial means (bank statements, employment contract, or pension documentation)
  • Health insurance valid in Albania
  • Clean criminal record certificate from your home country (apostilled and translated)
  • Category-specific documents (e.g., work contract, company registration, enrollment certificate)

All foreign documents must be apostilled and accompanied by a certified Albanian translation.

The Application Process

Applications are submitted through the e-Albania online platform or in person at the relevant district office. The standard steps are:

  1. Gather and authenticate all required documents
  2. Complete the online application on e-Albania
  3. Attend the appointment at the Migration Directorate office if required
  4. Pay the applicable government fee
  5. Collect your residence permit card once approved

Processing typically takes 30 to 60 days, though timelines can vary based on the permit category and office workload.
