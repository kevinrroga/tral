Does Albania Require a Visa for Turkish Citizens?

Short answer: no. Turkish citizens can enter Albania visa-free with a valid passport and stay up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Staying longer, working, or settling changes things — here's the full picture.

How the 90-Day Visa-Free Period Works

Under the bilateral visa-exemption agreement between Turkey and Albania, Turkish citizens can stay in Albania up to 90 days within any 180-day period, visa-free. That allowance can be used as one continuous trip or split across multiple entries — what matters is that the total stays under 90 days inside the rolling 180-day window. The same exemption applies to Azerbaijani citizens.

When You Need a Visa or Permit Instead

The 90-day visa-free allowance covers short, tourist-style visits only. You'll need a residence permit (combined with a work permit where relevant) if you plan to:

  • Stay longer than 90 days
  • Work or take up employment in Albania
  • Start a company or operate as self-employed
  • Bring your spouse and children to join you (family reunification)
  • Settle long-term, including through property ownership

What Happens If You Overstay

Overstaying the visa-free period can mean a fine on exit, complications with future visa and residence permit applications, and in some cases a temporary entry ban. If you plan to stay longer, work, or settle, start the residence permit process before your visa-free time runs out — ideally within your first few weeks in Albania.

Passport and Other Requirements

  • Your passport should be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned departure from Albania
  • Airlines may ask to see a return or onward ticket
  • Entry rules can change; confirm the current position before you travel
